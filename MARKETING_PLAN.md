# F&D Staging - Comprehensive Marketing Plan 2024-2025

## Executive Summary

This marketing plan is designed to maximize F&D Staging's business growth in the San Francisco Bay Area luxury home staging market. The strategy focuses on high-value lead generation, brand positioning, and conversion optimization across multiple channels.

**Key Objectives:**

- Increase qualified leads by 150% within 12 months
- Establish F&D Staging as the #1 luxury staging company in the Bay Area
- Generate $2M+ in new business through digital marketing channels
- Build a sustainable referral network with top real estate agents
