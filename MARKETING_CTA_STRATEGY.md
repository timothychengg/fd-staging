## 🎨 CTA Design Best Practices

1. **Primary CTA:** Large, high-contrast button (current `btn-pill` style works well)
2. **Secondary CTA:** Outlined button or text link
3. **Tertiary CTA:** Subtle text link

**Color Psychology:**

- Use your `luxtxt` (dark) for primary CTAs - conveys trust and professionalism
- Use `luxaccent` for hover states - draws attention
- Maintain luxury aesthetic - avoid overly aggressive colors

**Copy Guidelines:**

- ✅ Lead with benefit/value
- ✅ Use action verbs (Get, Start, Book, See)
- ✅ Include numbers when possible (48%, 286%, etc.)
- ✅ Remove friction words ("Free", "No Obligation")
- ✅ Match CTA to page context

**Examples of High-Converting Copy:**

- "Get Your Free ROI Estimate" (removes risk)
- "Sell 48% Faster - Get Started" (specific benefit)
- "Book Your Consultation (No Commitment)" (removes risk)
- "See How We Staged This Property" (curiosity-driven)
